The development

Castlegate, Prestbury

A detached four-bedroom home in warm brick, with sandstone dressings and a natural slate roof. Two gables frame a full-height glazed entrance, so you can see straight through the hall to the garden.

Downstairs there's a kitchen with dining and living space, a formal lounge, a study, a pantry, a utility room, cloaks and a WC. Upstairs, the principal bedroom has a dressing room, an en-suite and a Juliet balcony, and each of the other three bedrooms has its own en-suite.

The build

From bare ground to handover

Scroll to see the house go up

  1. 01

    Setting out

    Levels are checked on site and the footprint is pegged out against the approved drawings.

  2. 02

    Groundworks

    The foundations are dug and poured, services go in, and a damp-proof course is laid at ground-floor level.

  3. 03

    Superstructure

    The brickwork goes up one course at a time, with sandstone banding and capping where the drawings call for it.

  4. 04

    Roof

    The trusses go up first, then natural slate tiles, the chimney and PPC aluminium rainwater goods.

  5. 05

    Windows & doors

    Slim PPC aluminium frames are fitted with sandstone heads and cills, along with the full-height glazed entrance.

  6. 06

    Landscape & handover

    The scaffold comes down, the hedging goes in and the house is handed over, finished just as it was drawn.

Approved design

Four sides, one character

The walls are brick with sandstone capping, and the roof is slate with PPC aluminium rainwater goods. Every window and door has a PPC aluminium frame with sandstone heads and cills. A bat box and a sparrow box are built into the walls.

Interiors

Dark, warm and enveloping

Inside, the house is low-lit and tactile, finished in walnut, travertine, honed marble, olive velvet and unlacquered brass. Each room is built around one dramatic stone, with quieter materials around it.

01

Dark and enveloping

Colour goes on the walls, ceilings and joinery rather than on the cushions. The lighting stays low throughout.

02

Stone does the talking

Calacatta Viola, Rosso Levanto and honed travertine. Each room gets one dramatic stone, with quieter finishes around it.

03

Texture, not pattern

Fluted walnut, zellige, bouclé, reeded glass and heavy plaster, with almost no print anywhere.

04

Warm metal only

All the metalwork is unlacquered brass and bronze, left to patinate. There's no chrome and no matt black.

Palette

Eight colours, one house

Roughly 60% warm darks, 30% stone and plaster, and 10% accent. Each room uses oxblood or olive as its accent, never both.

Our approach

Few homes, properly built

  1. I

    The right plot

    We look for sites where a single exceptional house belongs, rather than ones we can pack with homes.

  2. II

    Drawn properly

    We work with architects and interior designers so the brick, the stone and the light are decided together.

  3. III

    Built carefully

    Everything is checked on site and built to current British Standards, using materials chosen to age well.

  4. IV

    Handed over finished

    The landscaping is in, the brass is fitted and every room is ready to live in from the first day.
