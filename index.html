From group chat to booked weekend, in four steps.

1

Create a trip, share one link

Drop it in the group chat. No app, no account needed for anyone but you.

2

Everyone answers 3 quick questions — privately

Dates they can't do, what they can spend, and anything else. No one's budget gets put on the spot in front of the group.

3

The group votes on 2–3 real options

By a set deadline. No reply counts as a "yes" to the winner, so the trip never stalls on the quiet ones.

4

Deposits land in one pot, automatically

Kitty sends the reminders and chases the stragglers. You just show up.

Built so nobody feels awkward.

  • No signup for participants. Twelve accounts kills a group before the first vote. Your mates just click a link.
  • Private budgets. Nobody overstates what they can afford out of embarrassment, and nobody's loud opinion drowns out someone who's quietly priced out.
  • A deadline with a default. The single most important bit — it's what frees the organiser from chasing a decision.
  • Nagging comes from software, not from you. Automated, judgement-free reminders until the pot is full.

Questions people ask

Is there an app to download?

No. Kitty works straight in your phone's browser — no download, no update, no faff. Participants never need to install anything.

What about accommodation and itineraries?

Not yet. The first version is deliberately focused on getting the group to a decision and the deposit collected without drama. Booking and itineraries come later.

Is deposit money held safely?

Deposits are processed through a regulated UK payments partner rather than held by us directly. We'll publish full details before any pilot handles real money.

What information do you collect from participants?

Only what's needed to plan the trip — availability and budget. A plain-English privacy notice is shown before anyone enters anything, and there's no account required to use it.

This all sounds very early — is it live?

We're honest about where we are: this is pre-launch, and we're validating the idea with real groups by hand before writing a line of product code. Join the waitlist and we'll bring you in as it's ready.
